About

About the show

A panel talk show that looks at everyday issues through an unapologetically intersectional lens. Two hosts who grew up in different worlds sit down with people whose stories don't usually get on television, and pull complicated subjects apart with humour rather than jargon. Jess and Shaz have very different backgrounds and upbringings, and a strong friendship built on deep conversations about big ideas.

The pilot is a periods episode where the two hosts, a hijabi journalist and a bogan weirdo, a trans non-binary health promotion officer and a culturally aware period educator all talk about their own periods on free-to-air television. Community television is the only place that conversation can happen, which is brilliant, and why we made the show.

The Intersection is made by three women in Melbourne. It goes out on community television in Melbourne, Geelong and Adelaide, on streaming, and on social, which gets its own content.

Vision

A kind and tolerant society that shares compassion with people from all walks of life.

Mission

To create community television that reaches the hearts and minds of everyday people, breaks down ignorance, and fosters everyday kindness.

Values

  • Kindness At the heart of everything we do.
  • Respect We value the experiences of all people.
  • Inclusivity We make space for those often left out.
  • Intersectional feminism We uplift people in an inclusive way.
  • Authenticity We stay true to ourselves, our values and beliefs.
  • Gentle curiosity Openness and a desire to understand.
  • Optimism Believing in the possibility of a better, fairer future.
  • Bravery Willing to challenge norms and speak truth with care.
  • Unashamed Proud of who we are and the stories we share.

Who makes it

Shazma Gaffoor

Shaz is a producer and host of The Intersection, and a journalist covering health policy, cultural identity and social justice. She has produced vox pops for The Project, worked in sports broadcasting at the Australian Open, written health research features for La Trobe University, and writes regularly for Al Wasat Magazine.

Shazma's portfolio

Jessica Leonard

Jess is a producer and host of The Intersection, and a bogan weirdo with a background in health promotion communications. She teaches media, volunteers as a breastfeeding counsellor, and turns up for a lot of community arts.
